My Darling Marie,

20 months. That's 608 days of loving you. 608 days of you being the first thing on my mind when I wake up and the last thought before I sleep.

I know things haven't been easy lately. I know we've had our struggles, our misunderstandings, our moments of doubt. And I know that sometimes, love isn't enough — we also need patience, understanding, and the willingness to grow together.

But here's what I want you to know: I'm still here. I'm still choosing you. I'm still fighting for us.

Every challenge we face, every difficult conversation, every moment of uncertainty — they're all worth it because at the end of it, I still want to be with you. I still want to build a future with you. I still want to grow old with you.

You are not just my girlfriend. You are my best friend, my confidant, my safe place. You are the person who knows me better than anyone else, the one who has seen me at my worst and somehow still chooses to stay.

I'm not perfect. I make mistakes. I sometimes say the wrong things or fail to show you how much you mean to me. But please know that my love for you has never wavered. Not for a single moment.

So on our 20th monthsary, I'm not just celebrating another month together. I'm celebrating you — your strength, your patience, your love. I'm celebrating us — our resilience, our commitment, our story.

I love you, Marie. Today, tomorrow, and for all the tomorrows after that.

Forever and always yours,

Your Kevin 💕
